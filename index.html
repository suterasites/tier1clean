FAQ

Questions builders ask

Anything not covered here, email admin@tier1clean.com.au or call 0416 090 408.

The clean

  • What is a builders clean?

    It takes a completed build from construction state to handover state. Protection comes off, construction dust and residue come out, and every surface is cleaned back to the finish your client is expecting to walk into.

  • What is the difference between the three stages?

    Stage 1 is the full detailed clean of all areas including windows. Stage 2 is a re-clean of all areas after defects are rectified. Stage 3 is a final clean of all surfaces immediately prior to handover. Take all three or only the ones you need.

  • Do you clean windows and exterior areas as well?

    Yes. Windows, frames, tracks and fly screens are part of the standard clean. Exterior work covers car parks, basements, cladding, facades, alfresco areas, garages and entrances, with pressure washing and roof cleaning available on top.

  • Do you work on commercial or residential projects?

    Both. We work across commercial and residential builds, for builders and trade clients only.

Working with us

  • What areas do you cover?

    We are based in Melbourne and work across the metropolitan area. Send through the site address and we will confirm.

  • Can you work around our handover date?

    That is exactly why the clean is staged. Give us your practical completion, defects and handover dates and we will build the clean around your program.

  • Can we send you the plans for a quote?

    Yes, and it is the fastest way to get an accurate price. Attach your drawings to the form below with the site address and timeframe, and we will come back with a scoped quote.

  • Do you take private or domestic jobs?

    No. Tier1Clean is trade only. Working exclusively for builders keeps the team on construction programs instead of splitting time across domestic work.
